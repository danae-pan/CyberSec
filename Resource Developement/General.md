## Resource Developement

Focuses on how adversaries create,buy or compromise/steal resources they need to conduct their operations. It can incluse lawful or inlawful acquisition of network infrastucture, accounts or capabilities as exploit, tools and services for staging purposes. The resources can vary from usig purchased domains to support Command and Control (C&C) to compromising accounts utilising an existing persona or developing accounts to build personas from scratch. Specifically, based on MITRE ATT&CK Resource Development is comprised by the following 8 Techniques:

1. Acquire Access
2. Acquire Infrastructure
3. Compromise Accounts
4. Compromise Infrastructure
5. Develop Capabilities
6. Establish Accounts
7. Obtain Capabilities
8. Stage Capabilities

More about the Techniques and sub-Techniques on: 
https://attack.mitre.org/tactics/TA0042/
